Wages Over Time

What you would be paid today if you were paid in 1964 money.

Key dates (purple lines)

    How it's calculated

    • Silver: 1964 pay × 0.7234 oz per $1 (the silver in 1964 dimes, quarters and halves) × silver price
    • Gold: 1964 pay ÷ $35 per oz (the dollar's official gold value in 1964) × gold price
    • Inflation: 1964 pay × CPI ÷ 1964 CPI
    • 1964 pay: $2.53 average, $1.25 minimum. In 1964, silver coins held 93.5¢ of silver per $1.

    Sources

    • Silver, gold: World Bank monthly averages, then COMEX
    • Average wage: BLS, production and nonsupervisory
    • Minimum wage: US Dept. of Labor
    • Inflation: BLS CPI-U
